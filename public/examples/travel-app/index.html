travel-app

An invented trip-planning app for iOS, Android and the web: 21 screens in six areas (auth, home, trip, activity, sharing, settings) and eight flows.

Everything on this page is Starboard’s own output for examples/travel-app: starboard generate drew the wireframes and wrote the screen catalog, and the diagrams are the project’s Mermaid sources, drawn. The wireframes are grey boxes by design: they show each screen’s regions and their order, not a visual design.

App map

Section titled “App map”

Every screen, grouped by area, with the links between them (from generated/app-map.md). The screen catalog below lists each screen’s links in words.

Flows

Section titled “Flows”

Add an activity

add-activity · status: shipped · starts at trip.timeline

Goal: put something on one day of the trip, at a place.

Steps

Section titled “Steps”
  1. Tap @trip.timeline#add-activity.
    • If the traveller cannot edit logistics, the button is hidden.
  2. Name it in @activity.add#title.
  3. Choose the day in @activity.add#day.
  4. Pick a place in @activity.place-search#results.
  5. Tap @activity.add#save; the activity appears on @trip.timeline#days.

The flow’s source

Answer the inbox

answer-the-inbox · status: shipped · starts at trip.timeline

Goal: say whether you are going to the activities you were invited to, and bring someone else along.

Steps

Section titled “Steps”
  1. Open @trip.inbox from @trip.timeline#tabs.
  2. Tap an invitation in @trip.inbox#invitations to open @activity.detail.
  3. Answer in @activity.detail#answer; the invitation leaves the inbox.
  4. To bring someone along, tap @activity.detail#invite.
  5. Pick travellers in @activity.invite#travellers and tap @activity.invite#send; they show in @activity.detail#going as invited.

The flow’s source

Invite a traveller

invite-a-traveller · status: proposed · starts at trip.info

Goal: bring someone onto the trip by email, with the role they need.

Steps

Section titled “Steps”
  1. Tap @trip.info#people to open @sharing.roster.
  2. Tap @sharing.roster#invite.
    • Without the manageMembers capability, the button is hidden.
  3. Type their address in @sharing.invite#email.
  4. Choose what they may do in @sharing.invite#role.
  5. Tap @sharing.invite#send; they appear in @sharing.roster#people as invited.

Platform adaptations

Section titled “Platform adaptations”
  • android: @sharing.invite is proposed; until it ships, organizers on Android share the join code from @trip.info#join-code instead.

The flow’s source

Join a trip

join-a-trip · status: shipped · starts at home.trips

Goal: join a trip someone else planned, with the code they shared.

Steps

Section titled “Steps”
  1. Tap @home.trips#join.
  2. Type the code in @sharing.join#code.
  3. Tap @sharing.join#join.
    • If no trip has that code, @sharing.join stays open and says so.
  4. The trip’s @trip.timeline opens, and the trip is now in @home.trips#trips.

The flow’s source

Look around a trip

look-around-a-trip · status: shipped · starts at home.trips

Goal: see what is planned, day by day and on the map, without changing anything.

Steps

Section titled “Steps”
  1. Tap a trip in @home.trips#trips.
  2. On @trip.timeline, tap @trip.timeline#day-heading to see the whole day on @trip.day.
  3. Tap an activity in @trip.day#activities to open @activity.detail.
  4. Back on the timeline, switch to @trip.map from @trip.timeline#tabs.
  5. Tap @trip.timeline#info for the dates and places on @trip.info.
    • Once the trip is over, @trip.info#ended says so.

Platform adaptations

Section titled “Platform adaptations”
  • web-wide: the tabs are a sidebar, and @trip.timeline#day-map shows the day’s places beside the timeline.

The flow’s source

Pack for a trip

pack-for-a-trip · status: proposed · starts at trip.checklist

Goal: agree who brings what, so nothing is packed twice or forgotten.

Steps

Section titled “Steps”
  1. Open @trip.checklist from @trip.timeline#tabs.
  2. Tap @trip.checklist#packing.
  3. Add what to bring with @trip.packing#add-item.
  4. Each entry in @trip.packing#items says who packs it; tick it off once it is in a bag.

The flow’s source

Plan a trip

plan-a-trip · status: shipped · starts at home.trips

Goal: start a new trip and land on its timeline, ready for activities.

Steps

Section titled “Steps”
  1. Tap @home.trips#new-trip.
  2. Name the trip in @home.new-trip#name.
  3. Choose the dates in @home.new-trip#dates and the place in @home.new-trip#where.
  4. Tap @home.new-trip#create to open @trip.timeline, empty until the first activity is added.

The flow’s source

Sign up, sign in and sign out

sign-in-and-out · status: shipped · starts at auth.welcome

Goal: get from a signed-out app to your trips, check the account, and sign out again.

Steps

Section titled “Steps”
  1. Tap @auth.welcome#sign-up, or @auth.welcome#sign-in with an account already.
  2. To sign up, fill in @auth.sign-up#details and tap @auth.sign-up#create.
  3. To sign in, fill in @auth.sign-in#credentials and tap @auth.sign-in#sign-in.
  4. Either way @home.trips opens, empty until you plan or join a trip.
  5. Tap @home.trips#settings to open @settings.main.
  6. See your name and email on @settings.account, opened with @settings.main#account.
  7. Back on @settings.main, tap @settings.main#sign-out to return to @auth.welcome.

Platform adaptations

Section titled “Platform adaptations”
  • web: there is no account screen; step 6 happens in @settings.main#profile.
