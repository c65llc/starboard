AGPL and you

Starboard is free software: Copyright © 2026 C65 LLC, licensed under the GNU Affero General Public License, version 3 only (AGPL-3.0-only). Everything in the repository is under it: the starboard command, the WebAssembly core, the web app at /app/, the GitHub Action and the agent skills.

The AGPL is about Starboard itself: what you may do when you copy, change or pass on Starboard, or offer a changed version of it to people over a network. It puts no condition on running Starboard on your own project.

This page is a plain-language summary, not legal advice. The license text below is what counts; for a question about your own situation, ask your counsel.

Questions and answers

Section titled “Questions and answers”

Can I use Starboard on a closed-source product?

Section titled “Can I use Starboard on a closed-source product?”

Yes. Running Starboard, on your machine, in CI or through the GitHub Action, is use, and section 2 of the license affirms your “unlimited permission to run the unmodified Program”. Your product’s code does not become AGPL because a tool checked its screen reference.

Are my screens.yaml, flows and generated pages covered by the AGPL?

Section titled “Are my screens.yaml, flows and generated pages covered by the AGPL?”

The files you write are yours, under whatever terms you choose. Section 2 also says the output of running Starboard is covered by the license only if that output, given its content, is itself a covered work. The wireframes, the app map and the screen catalog are drawn from your files and contain no part of Starboard’s program.

What about the agent skills that starboard skills install writes?

Section titled “What about the agent skills that starboard skills install writes?”

The skills are Starboard’s own text, under the same license. starboard skills install copies them into your repository, adapting only their front matter for each agent. Keeping them beside your code does not change your code’s license. If you change the skills and pass them on, the changed skills stay under the AGPL.

I changed Starboard. What do I have to do?

Section titled “I changed Starboard. What do I have to do?”

Nothing, if you only run the changed version yourselves. If you give it to anyone else, you pass it on under the AGPL, with its source. If you change Starboard and let people use it over a network, for example a hosted copy of the web app, section 13 asks you to offer those users the source of your changed version.

Can I contribute?

Section titled “Can I contribute?”

Yes. CONTRIBUTING.md explains how. Every contribution, once accepted, is licensed under the same terms, AGPL-3.0-only.
