unknown-platform

What it catches

Section titled “What it catches”

A platform name in a screen that is not a key under platforms in starboard.yaml. It is checked in the screen’s platforms list, and in the platform keys of its status, files and screenshots mappings.

Why it matters

Section titled “Why it matters”

A platform Starboard does not know has no form factors to draw and no source roots to check, so whatever the screen says about it is never checked. It is usually a typo (IOS, andriod) or a platform that was renamed or removed in starboard.yaml. This is a correctness rule: it is always an error.

How to fix

Section titled “How to fix”

Correct the name to one of the configured platforms, or add the platform under platforms in starboard.yaml.
